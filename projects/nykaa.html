Nykaa teardown

Shoppers finish the decision outside the app.

Product teardown, beauty e-commerce. Full title: Why shoppers finish the decision outside Nykaa

  • Research
  • 3 of 4shoppers I observed checked options outside Nykaa before deciding
  • 575+lip balms I started from
  • 456+left after choosing Balm and Stick
  • 4products under the Dermatologically Tested filter (5 on a later check)

The problem

  • I picked one lip balm from 575+ options on Nykaa and tracked every step as smooth, confusing or slow. I also spoke to three other shoppers.
  • Three of the four of us checked options outside the app before deciding, two of them using ChatGPT.
  • That is four people, one category and no analytics data, so I read the result as directional, not statistical.

What I did

  • I walked six steps from browsing to the cart. Choosing Balm and Stick cut 575+ products to 456+. The Dermatologically Tested filter left only 4 (5 on a later check), and a product I know qualifies appeared only through search.
  • I interviewed three users, aged 27, 25 and 27, in Hubli and Chennai, with open questions and follow-ups when an answer was unclear.
  • I counted who raised each point out of four, and kept the positive signals (Most Reordered, ratings, offers, direct sourcing) apart from the friction.

The decision

  • The root cause I settled on: Nykaa gives data but not answers. Is this list complete, which one fits me, will this shade suit me?
  • Three bets to keep the decision on Nykaa: make filters complete and honest (now), show every shade worn on lips (next), and compare shortlisted products side by side (next).
  • I held three things back on purpose: a brand that is not listed is a supply-side call, delivery and tracking needs more users, and checkout lag came from one user, so performance data comes first.
  • Each bet has a cheapest test and a stop rule. The filter test is to check 50 products that brands list as dermatologically tested against the filter.

What it shows about how I work

  • Reading a small sample honestly: I say who raised each point out of four and what four people cannot tell us.
  • Following up on unclear answers. The wishlist question came from one, and it showed that a brand that is not listed cannot be wishlisted.
  • This is a diagnosis with proposed tests. Nothing here has been built or measured.
