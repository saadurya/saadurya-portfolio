VitaFit Connect & Commit

Two features aimed at one retention problem.

PRD, fitness app. Full title: Connect & Commit: rewards and challenges for VitaFit

  • Specs
  • Growth
  • 25% → 32%share of active users doing 3+ workouts a week: baseline to the week 6 beta target (planning figures)
  • +4 ptsNorth Star lift against control needed at week 10 to go to Phase 2
  • 2,000users per arm (planning figure, confirmed by a power check in week 1)
  • 20 mina week per instructor, held as a guardrail

The problem

  • On-demand users lose motivation after the first weeks, train alone and have no reason to prefer one instructor over another. Nothing today brings them back on a day they do not feel like it.
  • Public research points the same way. In a 2009 IHRSA / HCD / Les Mills survey of 2,200+ US and Canadian club members, group-class participants attended twice as often as other members, and typical health and fitness apps keep only 3 to 8% of users at day 30.
  • Internal signals are confirmed in week 1 before any build, so the baselines in the PRD are planning figures.

What I did

  • I wrote a PRD for two features aimed at one retention problem: Gamification & Rewards, and Community Challenges. The loop is workout, progress, instructor note, challenge, return.
  • I specified what a build needs: points rates, streak rules including the broken state, a weekly instructor batch capped at 25 members each, and 7-day challenges where 5 of 7 days counts as complete.
  • I planned an A/B test on one segment, working professionals aged 25 to 35, with flows and wireframes on pages 3 to 5.

The decision

  • Instructor recognition is the differentiator: a short personal note at milestones, within a budget of 20 minutes a week per instructor, tested with 5 instructors in week 3.
  • A decision rule for week 10: go to Phase 2 only if the North Star is up 4 points, 30-day retention is up 4 points and instructor recognition completion stays at 70% or more. Stop everything if the North Star lift is under 2 points.
  • I cut free-text replies (moderation cost), cash-value rewards (margin), per-session shout-outs (does not scale) and the Gen Z test, which is Phase 2.

What it shows about how I work

  • Tying metrics to behaviour, with a North Star, guardrails and a stop rule.
  • Writing down what the feature will not fix: churn from content quality, price or injury.
  • Planning to confirm internal numbers before building.
