LEAD CASE · 0 → 1

Raise: a live app for a parent's hardest first sentence, 0 → 1.

A live app that helps a parent of a 14 to 18-year-old start a hard conversation with a short plan they can edit, then asks what happened.

CAPSTONE · AIRTRIBE PM PROGRAM · OCTOBER 2026 · LIVE APP

  1. 28 + 15 + 31
    Who I asked

    28 parents and 15 teenagers on my form, and 31 interviews. They are mostly people I know, so I read the results as these parents, not parents in general.

  2. 214
    Tests passing on the rebuild

    The rebuild is live and was built in sixteen tasks, one commit each.

  3. 30 of 30
    Concerns answered on the final release-test run

    The first run on the real AI answered 27 of 30. I fixed the failures and ran it again.

  4. 15 of 20
    Plans with no flaw, below my 95% bar

    I reviewed 20 of the 30 plans. None was unsafe, and 5 had a minor tone or copy flaw.

  5. 40%
    The target for completed conversations

    From 20+ parents, 14 days after each plan. It is a target: results from real parents on the rebuild are yet to be measured.
