How I work

Find the real problem

I start with users and evidence: interviews, public data, my own use of the product. I say how big the sample is and what it can't tell us.

Write it so a team can build it

PRDs with acceptance criteria, metrics and clear boundaries, and trade-offs agreed with engineering before work starts.

Use AI where it earns its place

I've built Zapier and n8n workflows, custom GPTs and Claude-based agents, and I test them by trying to break them.
