HOW I THINK

Across the fourteen case studies and Raise, I keep returning to four habits.

  1. Say what the evidence can't tell you.Raise rests on 28 parents, 15 teenagers and 31 interviews, and I read the results as those parents, not parents in general. In the Zomato analysis, every figure is marked as sourced, calculated or a planning figure.
  2. Sequence the bets.For Airbnb India I funded one growth loop first, not four, because it is cheap to test and produces the evidence the others need. In GymBuddy I held AI personalisation back until there is behaviour data to justify it.
  3. Put each decision where its failure can be caught.In Orbitly, format goes in skills, volume in agents and risk in code.
  4. Test it until it fails, then fix it.I ran 30 test concerns on Raise's real AI. It failed three, I fixed it and ran it again until all 30 passed.
