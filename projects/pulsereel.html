PulseReel Circuit Breaker

Slow fast-mobilising reels in proportion, with a person in the loop.

PRD, short-video platform in India. Full title: PulseReel Circuit Breaker

  • Specs
  • 72 hoursfor one unverified reel to become a nationwide protest movement, the case behind the PRD
  • 45 of 50mobilization-related reels the Phase 0 backtest must catch at L2 or above before launch (V1 setting)
  • 3 of 150ordinary reels allowed at L3 or above in that backtest (V1 setting)
  • 80%week 12 target: confirmed mobilizing reels limited before 250,000 views (V1 setting)

The problem

  • PulseReel's 60M users are all aged 18 to 25, and its recommender ranks reels by how fast people engage. That cannot tell a reel people watch from a reel that makes people gather.
  • In the brief's case, one unverified “incident” reel became a nationwide protest movement in 72 hours, and the only options were full amplification or removal.
  • If the reel is false, amplifying it risks panic and violence. If it is true, cutting it silences a legitimate protest.

What I did

  • I wrote a V1 PRD: problem statement, root causes by 5 whys, pain points with public evidence, user stories for analysts, viewers and creators, and functional requirements. Each number is tagged as sourced, calculated or a V1 setting.
  • I designed a Mobilization Score from 0 to 100 and a ladder from Normal to Hold, where every step ends on a timer.
  • I planned a Phase 0 backtest on 200 labelled past reels, 50 mobilization-related and 150 ordinary top-velocity ones, before anything launches.

The decision

  • Detect spread patterns, never political viewpoints. Reach is cut only when a reel does all three: spreads unusually fast, asks people to gather at a time and place, and makes an unverified first-hand claim of harm. Popularity and political topic never reduce reach.
  • Keep a person in the loop. The strongest steps are reviewed within 30 minutes and 2 hours, and a second reviewer audits every L3 and L4 case weekly.
  • Hold firm boundaries: V1 does not decide whether an incident is real, remove content, or strike or ban creators.

What it shows about how I work

  • Writing a spec with a number on every rule, and a backtest before launch.
  • Firm boundaries about what the system is not: an investigation or a fact-checking tool.
  • Success metrics with guardrails for over-action, fairness and audit completeness, not just the headline 80%.
